history: Chassis no. 1643, one of just 1,744 Porsche 911 S examples produced for the 1970 model year, was discovered in Northern Italy after spending approximately twenty years stored in a garage. Remarkably complete and well preserved, it provided the basis for the comprehensive specialist restoration that followed. Today, retaining its matching-numbers engine, correct gearbox and original factory colour, it represents a particularly authentic example of the early 911 S.

description:
This 1970 Porsche 911 S 2.2 Coupé is one of the most desirable early long-wheelbase 911s, combining the legendary 2.2-litre flat-six with the performance and purity that made the S models so highly regarded. This example retains its original Type 911/02 engine with matching chassis and engine numbers, together with the correct Type 911/01 gearbox and restored forged 6×15 Fuchs wheels.

Finished in its original and highly sought-after Tangerine 018, this 911 has undergone a meticulous two-year restoration by a renowned Porsche specialist. Stripped back to bare metal, the body was refinished to factory specifications, while the interior, mechanicals, suspension, steering and braking systems were carefully restored. Particular attention was paid to originality and detail throughout. The car is accompanied by photographic restoration documentation, its owner's manual, original tool kit and correct spare wheel.
