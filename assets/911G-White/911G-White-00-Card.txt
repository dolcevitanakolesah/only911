history: Built on December 1, 1973, and delivered new in Lucca on May 31, 1974, the car enjoyed a long Italian ownership history, passing through Reggio Emilia, Parma and Turin before arriving in Milan in 1984. One of its long-term owners was President of Porsche Club Italia. The current owner acquired the car in 2019, and it retains its original Milan registration. 

description:
One of the most fascinating early G-Series 911s, the 1974 Carrera 2.7 is the direct heir to the legendary 2.7 Carrera RS. Its iconic 2.7-litre flat-six with Bosch mechanical fuel injection (MFI) delivers a truly distinctive driving experience.

Finished in its original Grand Prix White with blue-black Shetland Twill interior, this matching-numbers example has undergone a conservative restoration by Porsche Classic Centre Catania. Accompanied by its original equipment, extensive documentation and Porsche Classic Certificate of Origin, it features period-correct Carrera lettering, Pirelli CN36 tyres and ducktail spoiler. A rare early-production detail is the specific central-tunnel reinforcement fitted to the first approximately 800 examples.
