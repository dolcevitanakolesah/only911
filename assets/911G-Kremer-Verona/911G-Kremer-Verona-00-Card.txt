An exceptional 1981 Porsche 911 SC 3.0, showing just 1,120 km on the odometer. The car retains its original engine and gearbox and features the distinctive Kremer “Turbo-Umba” body kit, complemented by modified wheels and rims fitted with spacers. Its unmistakable period styling gives it a unique presence while capturing the spirit of the early 1980s Porsche era. 
 
The interior has been completely reupholstered, featuring modified fabrics and trim that give the cabin a distinctive character. Mechanically, the car has undergone a comprehensive overhaul, with all work documented and supported by the relevant invoice. It is accompanied by the prestigious ASI Targa Oro certification and is currently part of the collection of a dedicated Porsche enthusiast who owns more than 50 Porsche automobiles.
