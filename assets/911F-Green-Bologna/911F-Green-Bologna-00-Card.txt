history: Produced on 4 July 1969 for the Italian market, the car was delivered new in Florence in September 1969 to a prominent entrepreneur. After spending time in Milan, it passed to its previous owner in 1990. Following its restoration, in 2016, it was sold to its current owner. A matching-numbers example, it comes with its Porsche Production Certificate and ASI registration. 

description:
This 1969 Porsche 911 2.0 E is a particularly desirable early long-wheelbase model, benefiting from improved handling, mechanical fuel injection and a 140 hp flat-six engine. Sharing much of its specification with the range-topping S, it features ventilated brakes, aluminium calipers and 6x15 Fuchs wheels, offering an excellent balance of performance and refinement.

Finished in its original Irish Green over a black vinyl and velour interior, this example has undergone a comprehensive restoration carried out with great attention to originality. The body retains its original floor pans, while the mechanicals, electrical system and interior have all been fully refurbished. The cabin also features a distinctive pair of Recaro sports seats upholstered in white, black and green houndstooth fabric.
