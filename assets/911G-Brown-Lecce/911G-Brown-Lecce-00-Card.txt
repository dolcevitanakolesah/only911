history: An Italian car from the very beginning, this 911 started its life in Livorno, where it was originally sold by Gesicar Porsche. At a later stage, it changed ownership and moved to Padova, where it was maintained by Centro Assistenza Porsche Padova Est. On 20 December 2023, the car was purchased by its current owner in Padova and subsequently brought south to Lecce, in the beautiful region of Puglia. Its entirely Italian history, original paintwork and ownership journey make it a particularly interesting example of the 911 SC.
 
description:
A true expression of the golden era of Porsche, this 1981 Porsche 911 SC 3.0 embodies everything that makes the classic 911 so special. Powered by the iconic 3.0-litre flat-six engine and paired with a manual gearbox, it delivers the unmistakable character, sound and driving experience that have made the 911 a timeless sports car. With 180,000 km on the clock, this example retains its original colour and factory paintwork, a testament to its well-preserved condition.
 
Presented in remarkable shape, the car features an opening sunroof, rear spoiler and distinctive all-black alloy wheels, giving it a particularly striking appearance while respecting its original character. The tyres have recently been replaced, and the mileage is certified and guaranteed. Described as being in near-new condition, this 911 SC offers a wonderful opportunity to experience one of Porsche's most iconic air-cooled models.
