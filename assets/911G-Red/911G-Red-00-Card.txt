history: Delivered new in 1989, the car spent its first 30 years in the United States before arriving in Europe, where it was converted externally to Rest of World specifications. description:
The final evolution of the legendary G-Series, the 1989 911 Turbo Cabriolet combines the iconic 3.3-litre turbocharged flat-six with the highly desirable G50 five-speed gearbox and hydraulic clutch. With 282 hp and 377 Nm of torque, it offers an engaging yet more progressive driving experience. One of fewer than 900 examples produced worldwide, it is among the rarest and most desirable G-Series Turbos. 
 
This example is equipped with a rich factory specification, including limited-slip differential, sport steering wheel, short shifter, Blaupunkt radio and amplifier system. Presented in excellent condition, it features refreshed bodywork, recently renewed front seats and a well-preserved interior. The last service was carried out at Porsche Centre Latina, and no immediate work is required.
