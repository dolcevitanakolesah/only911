history: Sold new in Michigan on July 28, 1989, to its first and only American owner, the car remained in his care for 31 years, covering approximately 35,000 miles. It was subsequently acquired by its current Italian owner, who brought it back to Europe. Converted aesthetically to European specifications while retaining its original components, it has covered fewer than 2,000 miles in the last four years. Accompanied by its original service book, Carfax report, tool kit, jack, compressor, booklets and rare protective cover for the folded rear window. 
 
description:
The 1989 911 Speedster represents the ultimate evolution of the classic G-Series, combining its distinctive low windshield, minimalist interior and iconic soft top with the mechanical excellence of the Carrera 3.2. Equipped with the G50 five-speed gearbox, limited-slip differential and Turbo-derived braking system, it offers an engaging open-air driving experience. This US-specification example delivers 217 hp. 
 
Finished in elegant Black over Linen leather, it features an attractive factory specification including air conditioning, cruise control, electrically adjustable seats and limited-slip differential. The car retains its original engine and is presented in excellent condition, with beautifully preserved interior, hood and mechanics, recently replaced tyres and complete onboard equipment.
